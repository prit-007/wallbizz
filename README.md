# WALLBIZZ

**Premium curated wallpapers. Zero clutter. Pure aesthetic.**

Wallbizz is a dark-first wallpaper app powered by Wallhaven's top-rated 4K wallpapers. Browse seven curated categories, search millions of wallpapers, download to your device, and build personal moodboard collections — all with a Vogue-editorial glassmorphism UI.

> **Web PWA** + **Android** — [Try it live](https://wallbizz.netlify.app)

## How It Works

1. **Browse** — Open the app and explore seven curated categories. The staggered grid shows wallpapers at their native aspect ratio.

2. **Search** — Tap the search bar to find any wallpaper. Use filters for sort order, date range, category, and aspect ratio.

3. **Save** — Tap the heart icon to add wallpapers to your collection. Create moodboards to organize favorites into themed groups.

4. **Download** — Save any wallpaper to your device. Downloads are tracked in the Vault tab for offline browsing.

5. **Share** — Share wallpapers with Wallbizz branding via your device's native share sheet.

6. **Set Wallpaper** — Download any wallpaper and set it directly as your device background.

## Features in Detail

### Curated Categories
Seven hand-picked categories refresh daily from Wallhaven's top-rated wallpapers:

- **Trending** — The most popular wallpapers right now
- **Anime** — Top-rated anime artwork
- **Nature** — Landscapes, forests, oceans, and wildlife
- **Cyberpunk** — Neon-lit cityscapes and sci-fi worlds
- **Space** — Galaxies, nebulas, planets, and cosmic scenes
- **Desktop** — Optimized for 16:9 and 16:10 monitors
- **Mobile** — Optimized for 9:16 and 10:16 phones

### Smart Search
Search millions of wallpapers with powerful filters:

- **Sort:** Toplist, Latest, Most Viewed, Most Favorited, Random
- **Date Range:** All Time, Past 24h, Past Week, Past Month, Past Year
- **Category:** All Types, General, Anime, People
- **Aspect Ratio:** All, Mobile (9:16), Desktop (16:9), Square (1:1)

Popular search tags: Cyberpunk, Studio Ghibli, AMOLED, Neon City, Minimalist, Pixel Art, Space, Gothic

### Moodboard Collections
Create named moodboard collections to organize your favorite wallpapers:

- Create unlimited moodboards with custom names
- Add or remove wallpapers from any moodboard
- Browse moodboard contents in a masonry grid
- Swipe to delete wallpapers from a moodboard
- Delete entire moodboards with confirmation

### Download Vault
Every wallpaper you download is saved to your Vault:

- Browse all downloads in a responsive grid
- Search downloads by name, color, resolution, or category
- Set any downloaded wallpaper as your device background
- Remove downloads you no longer need
- Download count shown as badge on the nav bar

### Glassmorphism UI
Every element is designed with a dark-premium aesthetic:

- Frosted glass navigation bar with backdrop blur
- Dynamic theming that tints to the wallpaper's primary color
- Smooth animations on every interaction
- Pure black native splash screen
- Responsive layout from phone to desktop
